    Entity Resolution Decision Framework

    Sherlock AI resolves identities across multiple documents into three explicit outcomes:

    MATCH

    Sufficient corroborated evidence (co-occurring relationships, shared identifiers, verified attributes) supports that both mentions refer to the same real-world identity.

    REVIEW

    Evidence is ambiguous, partial, or conflicting. Automated merging is halted; human investigator adjudication with documented rationale is required.

    NON-MATCH

    Contradicting attributes (distinct birthdates, tax IDs, incompatible jurisdictions) or explicit analyst constraints rule out identity merging.
